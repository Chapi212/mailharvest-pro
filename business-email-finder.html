Glossary

EMAIL FINDER GLOSSARY

Key terms every sales professional should know when evaluating business email finder tools.

MX Validation
Checking a domain's mail exchange (MX) DNS records to confirm it can receive email. MailHarvest Pro performs dual MX validation against Google DNS and Cloudflare DNS simultaneously for every found address.
Hot / Warm / Cold Scoring
AI classification of a business email by contact seniority. Hot = C-Suite, Founders, Directors and VPs. Warm = Managers, team leads, sales contacts. Cold = generic inboxes like info@ and no-reply@.
Live Extraction
Finding business emails directly from a webpage as it loads, rather than querying a pre-built database. MailHarvest Pro extracts live — every email reflects the current state of that page today.
Bulk URL Automation
Pasting a list of company URLs and letting MailHarvest Pro extract, validate and score every business email found across all pages automatically — up to 1,000 URLs per run.
Cloaked Email
An email address disguised to avoid scrapers — e.g. name [at] company [dot] com. MailHarvest Pro decodes all common cloaking formats automatically.
CRM-Ready Export
The structure a company uses for employee emails — e.g., first.last@company.com, firstinitiallast@company.com, or first@company.com. MailHarvest Pro discovers patterns by domain and applies them to find new contacts.
Disposable Domain
A CSV file formatted with columns that map directly to CRM fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Company, Source URL, Score, AI Score, Domain and Date — no manual column mapping needed.
Document Extraction
Finding all email addresses associated with a specific company domain. MailHarvest Pro discovers patterns and generates verified emails for any person at that company, even if they are not in public databases.
Shadow DOM
Appending missing email addresses to existing contact records. MailHarvest Pro can take a list of names and companies with missing emails and fill in the gaps with verified, scored addresses.
Dual DNS Validation
Checking an email's domain mail records against two independent DNS resolvers — Google DNS and Cloudflare DNS — to confirm deliverability without exposing your IP to the target server.
