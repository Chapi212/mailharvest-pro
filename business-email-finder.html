Glossary

EMAIL FINDER GLOSSARY

Key terms every sales professional should know when evaluating business email finder tools.

SMTP Validation
The process of pinging a mail server to confirm an email address actually exists and can receive messages. ProspectIQ performs this in real-time for every lookup, ensuring under 3% bounce rates.
Pattern Matching
AI-driven prediction of email structure based on known formats from a company. If john.smith@company.com exists, the AI predicts jane.doe@company.com with high confidence. ProspectIQ uses 12M+ verified patterns.
Confidence Score
A reliability rating assigned to each found email. ProspectIQ uses three tiers: High (90%+), Medium (70-89%), and Low (below 70%). You always know exactly how trustworthy the data is before sending.
Bounce Rate
The percentage of emails that fail to deliver. Industry average for email finders is 12-20%. ProspectIQ maintains under 3% thanks to triple verification and real-time SMTP validation.
Bulk Lookup
Uploading a CSV file with multiple names/companies to find emails in batch. ProspectIQ processes up to 10,000 rows per upload on Growth plans and 50,000 on Enterprise. Results export to CSV or CRM.
Email Pattern
The structure a company uses for employee emails — e.g., first.last@company.com, firstinitiallast@company.com, or first@company.com. ProspectIQ discovers patterns by domain and applies them to find new contacts.
Human Verification
Manual checking of hard-to-find or high-value contacts by a research team. ProspectIQ applies human verification to all C-suite and executive emails, ensuring 100% accuracy for your most important outreach.
Domain Search
Finding all email addresses associated with a specific company domain. ProspectIQ discovers patterns and generates verified emails for any person at that company, even if they are not in public databases.
Email Enrichment
Appending missing email addresses to existing contact records. ProspectIQ can take a list of names and companies with missing emails and fill in the gaps with verified, scored addresses.
Catch-All Domain
A mail server configuration that accepts all emails sent to the domain, making SMTP validation unreliable. ProspectIQ detects catch-all domains and applies enhanced pattern matching and human verification for these cases.
