Glossary

LEAD ENRICHMENT GLOSSARY

Key terms every revenue professional should know when evaluating lead enrichment software.

Data Enrichment
The process of appending missing or outdated data to existing lead records. ProspectIQ enriches 50+ fields including contact info, firmographics, technographics, and intent signals.
Firmographics
Company characteristics like industry, size, revenue, location, and employee count. The foundation of B2B segmentation. ProspectIQ enriches firmographics from official registries with 96% accuracy.
Technographics
Data about the technologies a company uses — CRM, marketing automation, cloud infrastructure, etc. Reveals budget, maturity, and competitive landscape. ProspectIQ tracks 15,000+ technologies.
Contact Enrichment
Appending missing contact details to lead records: emails, phone numbers, social profiles, and job titles. ProspectIQ uses triple verification for emails and carrier validation for phones.
Auto-Enrichment
Automatic data append triggered by events — new lead creation, status changes, or time-based rules. ProspectIQ can enrich leads the moment they enter your CRM, with zero manual intervention.
Data Refresh
The frequency at which enriched data is updated. ProspectIQ refreshes every 4 hours. Most competitors update quarterly, meaning 3-6 months of stale data between refreshes.
Lead Scoring
A methodology for ranking leads by purchase likelihood. Enriched data feeds scoring models — company size, technology fit, intent signals, and job seniority all contribute to precise qualification.
CRM Hygiene
The practice of maintaining clean, accurate, and complete CRM data. ProspectIQ automates hygiene by identifying stale records, updating outdated fields, and appending missing data in bulk.
Enrichment API
A programmatic interface for enriching lead data at scale. ProspectIQ's REST API supports real-time single-record enrichment and bulk batch processing up to 50,000 records.
Confidence Score
A reliability rating for each appended data field. ProspectIQ scores every enrichment: High (90%+), Medium (70-89%), Low (below 70%). You always know how trustworthy your data is.
